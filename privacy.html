Privacy Policy

We built Ananda Manifest & Affirmation to help you build a daily positivity practice, and we try to collect only what's needed to make that work — please read on for the specifics.

1. Definitions

  • "App" means the Ananda Manifest & Affirmation mobile application, available on the Apple App Store and Google Play.
  • "Account" means the anonymous identity created for you the first time you use the App's personalization features.
  • "Personal Essence" means the free-text answers you provide about your past accomplishments and current challenges, used to personalize your affirmations.
  • "Service Providers" means third-party companies we use to operate the App (e.g. hosting, AI generation, subscription management).
  • "Device Identifier" means a non-permanent identifier tied to your installation of the App, not to you personally.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Account & Identity

Ananda Manifest & Affirmation does not require you to sign up with an email address or password. When you first use the personalization features (Settings → Personal Essence), the App creates an anonymous account via our backend (Supabase Authentication). This account is identified only by a randomly generated ID stored securely on your device (using iOS Keychain / Android Keystore-backed secure storage) — we do not collect your name as a login credential, and the account is not linked to an email address or phone number.

2.2 Personalization Content ("Personal Essence")

You may voluntarily provide:

  • First name (required) and last name (optional) — used to personalize a small number of your affirmations.
  • Affirmation mode (e.g. Daily Affirmation, Goals, Job Loss, Divorce, Family Issue, Manifestation) — the emotional focus you want your affirmations to have.
  • App language — one of 50 supported languages, used to generate and display affirmations in your preferred language.
  • Past milestones & anchors and current challenges & daily motivation — free-text descriptions (20–300 characters) you write about your own life, used solely to personalize the affirmations generated for you.

This content is stored in our database, associated only with your anonymous account, so the App can regenerate and refresh your affirmations without asking you to retype it every time.

2.3 Generated Content

The affirmations generated for you are stored against your anonymous account so they can be shown to you, saved as favorites, and re-displayed on your home screen widget.

2.4 Usage & Device Data

  • IP address — used transiently and automatically by our backend to rate-limit affirmation generation (to prevent abuse) and is not used for tracking, profiling, or advertising.
  • Device/app metadata — such as OS version and app version, collected automatically as part of standard network requests, for debugging and reliability.
  • Feature usage — such as your daily check-in streak, saved favorites, notification preferences, and whether you've placed the home screen widget — stored against your anonymous account to make those features work.

Ananda Manifest & Affirmation does not currently use any third-party analytics or advertising SDKs, and does not use your data for behavioral advertising or sell your personal information.

2.5 Notifications

If you enable daily affirmation reminders, notifications are scheduled locally on your device. We do not maintain a server-side push token database beyond what's strictly needed to deliver reminders you've opted into.

3. How Your Content Is Used to Generate Affirmations

To generate your personalized affirmations, your first name, affirmation mode, app language, and your Personal Essence text (accomplishments and challenges) are sent from our backend to a third-party AI language model provider (currently OpenAI's API) to produce the affirmation text. We do not send your account ID, IP address, device identifiers, or any purchase/subscription data to the AI provider — only the content described above, for the sole purpose of generating your affirmations. This content may be processed by the AI provider's infrastructure outside of your home country; see International Data Transfers below.

Please avoid entering sensitive information (such as health conditions, financial account details, or information about third parties) in the Personal Essence fields — they are meant for your own personal reflections and are processed by an AI system as described above.

4. How We Use Your Information

  • To generate and personalize your daily affirmations;
  • To operate core features: streaks, favorites, notifications, and the home screen widget;
  • To manage your subscription and unlock paid features you've purchased;
  • To maintain, secure, and improve the App (e.g. rate-limiting abuse, debugging errors);
  • To respond to support requests you send us; and
  • To comply with legal obligations.

We do not use your Personal Essence content, generated affirmations, or account data for advertising, and we do not sell your personal information to third parties.

6. Subscriptions, Purchases & Adapty (Refund Saver)

Ananda Manifest & Affirmation uses Adapty to manage in-app subscriptions and purchases on both iOS and Android, and to reconcile purchase events between the App Store/Google Play and our backend. As part of this, we use Adapty's Refund Saver feature, which helps legitimate subscription businesses like ours respond to fraudulent or abusive refund claims filed with Apple.

To make this possible, the following user-specific data is shared with Adapty and, where necessary, with Apple as part of the refund-request process:

  • Your anonymous account identifier and device/subscription identifiers (e.g. original transaction ID);
  • Purchase and subscription history (products purchased, purchase dates, renewal/cancellation/refund events, trial status, entitlement status);
  • App engagement signals relevant to demonstrating genuine use of the purchased features (e.g. whether the subscription's features were accessed after purchase); and
  • Store-provided metadata about the transaction (such as the App Store transaction and refund request identifiers).

This data is used only to verify legitimate purchases, manage your entitlements, and, where a refund request is contested, to provide Apple with evidence relevant to that specific request, consistent with Apple's App Store guidelines and StoreKit refund request APIs. It is not used for advertising or shared with any other third party for marketing purposes. You can review Adapty's own privacy practices at adapty.io/privacy.

7. Data Retention

  • Account & Personal Essence data — retained for as long as your account exists, until you request deletion.
  • Generated affirmations & favorites — retained alongside your account for as long as your account exists.
  • Subscription/purchase records — retained as required for financial record-keeping, fraud prevention, and refund-dispute purposes, even after account deletion, to the extent required by law or by our payment/subscription providers.
  • IP-based rate-limit records — retained only briefly (on a rolling window) to detect abuse, and are not linked to your account content.

8. Data Security

We use industry-standard measures to protect your information, including encrypted transport (HTTPS/TLS) for all network requests, encryption at rest for our hosted database, row-level access controls scoped to your account, and Keychain/Keystore-backed secure local storage for your session credentials on-device. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Your Rights & Choices

  • Delete your data — the App includes a "Delete My Data" option in Settings, which permanently removes your Personal Essence content, generated affirmations, and account data from our systems.
  • Access & correction — you can view and edit your Personal Essence content, affirmation mode, and language at any time in Settings.
  • Notifications — you can disable daily reminder notifications at any time in Settings or your device's system settings.
  • Data requests — you may contact us (see Contact Us) to request a copy of, or ask questions about, the data associated with your account.

If you are located in the European Economic Area, United Kingdom, or a U.S. state with a comprehensive privacy law (such as California, Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia, or Utah), you may have additional rights under applicable law, including the right to access, correct, delete, or port your personal information, and the right to opt out of certain processing. We do not sell personal information as defined under such laws.

10. International Data Transfers

Our primary database and backend infrastructure (Supabase) is hosted in the European Union. Content sent for AI processing (Section 3) and purchase-verification data sent to Adapty may be processed on servers located outside your country of residence, including in the United States. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards (such as our providers' standard contractual clauses) for these transfers.

11. Children's Privacy

Ananda Manifest & Affirmation is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required by your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under that age. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

12. App Store & Google Play Privacy Labels

The data categories disclosed in Ananda Manifest & Affirmation's Apple App Store "Privacy Nutrition Label" and Google Play "Data safety" section are kept aligned with the data practices described in this Policy (identifiers, user content, and purchase data, as applicable). If you notice any discrepancy, please let us know.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will update the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, notify you in the App. Continuing to use Ananda Manifest & Affirmation after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the revised Policy.
